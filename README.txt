Winona Thompson + Feisty: website prototype
===========================================

How to view
- Open index.html in a browser for the advice site.
- The Feisty shop is in feisty/index.html (also linked from the advice site footer).
- An internet connection is needed for the fonts. Everything else is in this folder.

How to share it
- Upload the whole folder to any web host, or drag it onto a drag-and-drop host such as Netlify Drop. Keep the folder structure as it is.

What's in it
- Advice site: home, two hubs (individuals and families, business), six service pages, about, insights, two articles, contact and booking, disclosures.
- Feisty: home, three kit pages, an item-by-item comparison, safety tips, our story.
- Working prototype pieces: mobile menu, FAQ accordions, booking picker, contact form, cart with colours and quantities. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Animations
- Sections fade in as you scroll, the line down the left of the advice pages draws itself, and the logo traces itself in on page heroes.
- Page-to-page fades work in Chrome and Edge.
- Everything switches off automatically for visitors who have reduced motion turned on.

Placeholders
- Anything in [square brackets] is for Winona to supply or confirm, such as the FSP number, testimonials and booking tool.
- Photos are taken from her current site. The service copy is her own, lightly edited.
