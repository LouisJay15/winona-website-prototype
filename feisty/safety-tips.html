Be aware. Be prepared. Be confident.

10 safety tips for everyday confidence.

Personal safety is about awareness, preparation and confidence. These ten tips help you stay alert, plan ahead and feel in control, whether you’re walking to your car, travelling for work, studying late or out with friends.

Be preparedBe confidentBe FeistyProtectPrepareEmpowerBe preparedBe confidentBe FeistyProtectPrepareEmpower
01

Be aware of your surroundings

Stay alert, especially in unfamiliar places. Keep your phone down and be present.

02

Trust your instincts

If something feels wrong, it probably is. Remove yourself from the situation.

03

Plan your route

Know where you’re going before you leave, and let someone know which way you’re heading.

04

Keep your phone ready

Keep it charged, with important numbers easy to reach. Share your location with someone you trust.

05

Travel smart

Use well-lit, busy routes. Avoid isolated areas, especially at night. Use reputable transport services.

06

Set boundaries

Be confident saying no. You don’t owe anyone your time, attention or personal space.

07

Learn basic self-defence

Simple techniques can give you the confidence and time to get to safety.

08

Keep essential safety tools

Carry a personal safety kit with tools like a personal alarm, whistle or other legal self-defence devices.

09

Stay connected

Keep in touch with family or friends. Share your location and let someone know when you arrive safely.

10

Look out for other women

Support and empower each other. A safer world is built when women look out for women.

How to use a personal safety alarm

One pull of the pin sets off a loud 130dB siren to attract attention and deter an attacker. Keep it on your car keys or handbag strap so it’s ready the moment you need it.

01

Pull out the pin

Hold the alarm in one hand and pull out the pin firmly.

02

The siren sounds

A loud 130dB siren draws attention and deters danger, buying you precious seconds.

03

Switch it off

Push the pin back in until it clicks.

04

Use the light

Press the button for a bright LED light in low light or at night.
