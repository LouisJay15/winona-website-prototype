Her story

Financial independence is only part of the journey

With a Postgraduate Diploma in Financial Planning Law (NQF8) and decades of industry experience, Winona believes financial advice should give people more than wealth. It should give them confidence, freedom and peace of mind.

Her vision is clear: to empower women to stand confidently on their own feet, financially, emotionally and physically. Every woman deserves the knowledge, resources and confidence to create her own future, protect herself and never feel powerless.

Whether she’s helping clients plan for retirement, protect their families, grow their investments or prepare their estate, her approach stays the same: honest advice, practical strategies and long-term partnerships built on trust.
